Rethinking Sumble's intelligence brief

I explored what happens to Sumble's AI-generated account brief once generating one costs credits: where reps would find it, how they decide it's worth paying for, and how to make the result clear and useful. This page collects the designs from that work, from flows and wireframes through to high fidelity.
